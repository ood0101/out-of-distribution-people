◆ The Alpha — two of them, and the second survives the uncertainty of the first

Alpha #1 — a founding-caliber AI-inference-silicon architect at a live departure. Woo is a named co-inventor (1 of 4, with Olivier Temam, Ravi Narayanaswami, and Google’s Edge-ML lead Harshit Khaitan) on the foundational “neural-network compute tile” patent (US 9,710,265), and holds the whole surrounding family — tensor-traversal unit, NN ISA, multi-dimensional tensor access, on-chip-resident parameters, input-sparsity exploitation, scheduling. That set is the architecture of an on-device inference accelerator. He shipped Edge TPU (Coral), the Pixel Neural Core, the Google Tensor SoC, and Gemini Nano (his last Google project) — then ran Samsung’s AGI Computing Lab as an SVP (the Mach-1 LLM-inference chip; the Gaia 4nm AI-PC accelerator). On pure pedigree he is more senior and more shipped-silicon than the ex-Google-TPU founders of MatX (Pope/Gunter were IC-level leads; Woo ran a chip org). The one thing that separated him from that founder cohort — that he kept choosing big-org leadership over founding — is exactly what changed this month.
