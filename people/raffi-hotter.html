◆ The Alpha — what’s actually special, measured honestly

Don’t read him as a solo physics genius — that’s not the edge, and the honest record won’t support it. The real, defensible alpha is an AND: a capable neuro-instrumentation / signal-processing builder who is also a serial neurotech-talent convener with unusual physics taste — and that catalysis just produced a ~5-person team that shipped a genuine world-first.

The thing that’s genuinely rare — the blog→company spine. Most founders backfill a thesis. Raffi did the opposite: in his 2022 post “Questions related to BCIs” he wrote down, verbatim, the exact wave-physics questions his company now answers — “What is the difference between optics and sound in wave propagation in the brain? Why is ultrasound attenuation lower…? How important is the diploe layer in scattering of ultrasound?” — then (his words) “spent much of the next year trying to answer them,” and built Aleph: super-resolution vascular imaging of a living brain through the intact skull via ultrasound localization microscopy (microbubbles + super-resolution), on Butterfly Network’s Ultrasound-on-Chip. His signature move is “software glasses for ultrasound” — fix transcranial aberration with computation (full-waveform inversion), not better hardware.

Calibrated honestly: the launch artifact is a team result — he’s the #2 committer on the ULM pipeline (substantive DSP/calibration: “noise levels in Pascals,” “scale the US forward model”), not the lead, and it’s high-res vascular imaging, not thought-decoding (telepathy is the aspiration). His Scholar record is thin by design (~41 cites, h-2) — he is deliberately a builder, not an academic; the spike is shipping + convening + taste, not citations or solo hardware dominance. The differentiator is breadth + execution speed + assembling the exact right people.
