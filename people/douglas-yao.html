The Science, honestly — a contrarian bet, not a de-risked drug

  • Mechanism = contrarian-but-arguable (not crank, not validated). Galanin is upregulated in the Alzheimer’s basal forebrain (Chan-Palay; Mufson; Counts) — but whether that’s pathological (his bet) or protective/compensatory is genuinely unresolved, and the field’s leading galanin-in-AD group (Mufson/Counts) reads it as protective and recommends agonists — the opposite of his antagonist. What rescues his thesis from the compensatory-vs-causal trap is a real, literature-grounded subtype argument: GalR1 is the inhibitory, ACh-suppressing, memory-impairing subtype; GalR2 is the trophic/protective one (Elliott-Hunt/Wynick 2007). So “block GalR1, spare GalR2” is coherent.
  • The load-bearing unproven assumption: his logic requires the basal-forebrain compensatory protection to be GalR2-mediated (so his drug spares it). That is not established; rodent localization (O’Donnell 1998) is messy — GalR1 sits on non-cholinergic cells, so the “antagonist disinhibits the cholinergic neuron” cartoon is indirect at best. If any protection runs through GalR1, the drug removes the compensation Mufson describes. And a hard, specific liability: GalR1-knockout mice develop spontaneous seizures and larger excitotoxic hippocampal lesions — chronic GalR1 blockade plausibly lowers seizure threshold in an AD population already at elevated seizure risk, directly threatening any “safer than donepezil” pitch.
  • The headline is an overclaim — false by his own citations. “World’s first selective GalR1 antagonist” — but the “dithiepine-1,1,4,4-tetroxide” he names as a prior failure is RWJ-57408, already characterized as a small-molecule selective GalR1 antagonist; Sch 202596 (Schering-Plough, 1997) was another. The defensible version — first drug-like, brain-penetrant selective GalR1 antagonist with in-vivo CNS efficacy — is real and is what the preprint supports. The gap is between the viral tweet and the paper.
  • The efficacy model is symptomatic, not disease-modifying. Donepezil-parity in a scopolamine-challenge Y-maze/NOR paradigm tests acute cholinergic boosting, not AD disease modification. Real (skeptical chemists on X demanded NMR/MS/HPLC purity data; HN called it “hobby project”) — independent QC/structure/assay confirmation is diligence item #1.
  • The one question that settles it (open with this): “What’s your evidence that the neuroprotective galanin signaling in the AD basal forebrain is GalR2-mediated rather than GalR1-mediated — and how do you de-risk lowering seizure threshold?” His answer tells you whether he’s thought past the trap or fallen into it.
