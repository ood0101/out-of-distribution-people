◆ Why tracking, not pursued — the AMD deal reframes the whole team

Taalas is being acquired by AMD. AMD announced a definitive agreement on Aug 6, 2026 (AMD IR release; terms undisclosed; "subject to customary closing conditions and regulatory approvals"; close expected Q4 2026; no completion notice in AMD’s IR feed as of Oct 27 — the definitive check is AMD’s Q3 results on Nov 3). Per EE Times, “Bajic and the Taalas team will join AMD under Vamsi Boppana’s AI organization” — a full acquisition, not an acquihire; Toronto team stays. No retention/earn-out terms are public.

So nobody here is a reach-out target today. They are a post-earn-out departure cohort: a closed 20+-year AMD-Markham → Tenstorrent → Taalas unit that has moved in lockstep at every transition, now re-entering AMD (which most of them left once already). The realistic inflection is a departure ~12–24 months after close (~2028). Indexed so the fund is pre-researched and first-call when it happens.
