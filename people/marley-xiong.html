◆ Investment Thesis — T0 (Active Pursuit)

This is a textbook out-of-distribution founder at an active inflection. Marley Xiong co-founded Aleph Neuro with Raffi Hotter and, per the team’s own account, “got 9 friends together and built a lab at home” to test two genuinely new imaging methods — acoustoelectric imaging and functional ultrasound through the skull. The payoff (June 2026): the world’s first 3D ultrasound-localization-microscopy image of a living human brain through an intact skull, resolving pial arteries and arterioles at a claimed ~100× the volumetric resolution of comparable CT — “MRI-level detail” without drilling or an MRI machine. Crucially, they released the processing code and the dataset on GitHub and published a technical write-up — this is a demonstrated result with artifacts, not a press-release superlative.
